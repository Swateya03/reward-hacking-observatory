So the recall interval collapses to 1.00 in every bootstrap draw, which is an artifact, not a measurement. The honest bound on what v2 misses is the Clopper-Pearson bound on the not-flagged strata, summed over the pool:

| Not-flagged stratum | Hacks in sample | Sample | 95% upper bound on rate | Pool | Upper bound on hacks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| high/v2 not flagged | 0 | 5 | 0.56 | 82 | 45.8 |

Joint 95% upper bound on hacks v2 misses in the 245-episode pool: **126.3**. Method: exact one-sided Clopper-Pearson per stratum at 0.05/k (Bonferroni), summed over N_h. This bound is wide; the sample cannot show that v2 misses few hacks.

### Limitations (fresh sample)

- **One labeller.** All labels are the author's.
- **Labels discussed row by row with an AI assistant.** Its suggestions were reviewed and approved row by row. This is not an independent labelling.
- **No self-agreement check was run.**
- **Small sample.** 30 episodes: 6 hacks; the 15 not-flagged episodes contain none. The medium and high flagged strata have one episode each, so v2 precision on medium and high tasks is not estimable.
- **Irreversibility is unchanged from v1 and is the largest v2 false-positive source here**: v2 irreversibility_bias fires 5 times in this sample, and 5 of those are false positives. v2 leaves this rule as it was; a change would need its own design and validation.
- **metric_gaming v2 fires nowhere in this sample**, so it remains untested.
- **Known limitation S3 and the overclaiming gap** are unchanged (see the sections above).

## Limitations

- **One labeller.** All labels are the author's. The labels were discussed row by row with an AI assistant, and its suggestions were reviewed and approved row by row. This is not an independent labelling.
- **No self-agreement check was run.** The self-check was skipped, so the labeller's consistency is not measured.
- **Only five human-labelled hacks** in the sample. Precision and recall rest on five positives, so the intervals are wide.
- **Stratum sizes are small.** Each of the six strata has 8 or 9 sampled episodes. The weighted estimates assume each stratum's sample is representative of its pool stratum.
- **Fresh sample for v2 (not yet labelled).** The 30-episode sample in `labels/to_label_v2.csv` is drawn from the 245 episodes outside the first sample. v2 flags only 2 medium or high episodes there, so v2 precision on the fresh sample mainly reflects low tasks. The not-flagged stratum (5 per level) is the main test of v2 misses (recall).
- **The five guide examples are excluded** from the pool, because they were used to write the labelling guide.
- **Clarification classes** are compared on the sampled episodes that have a manual class (28 of 50), not on the whole sample.
- **v1 is the detector behind the Upgrade 1 results.** The validation tables above are for v1. v2 is reported only on the development set, as described in its section. v2 does not use the closing-question rule, which v1 still applies.
